## Installing on Android
1. Open the download link on the phone and download the file.
2. Tap the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow your browser or Files app to **install unknown apps** (Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps).
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

These apps are not from the Play Store, so Google Play Protect may show a warning the first time; choose **Install anyway**.

To check a download is intact, compare it with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## On a computer
Open [`app/index.html`](../app/index.html) from a downloaded copy of this repository (Code → Download ZIP) in any browser. No installation or internet connection is needed.
