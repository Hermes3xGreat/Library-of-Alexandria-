# Sources and rights

- **Texts.** Most works are public-domain editions transcribed by Project Gutenberg volunteers and converted here. A few are public domain in the United States only (for example Summers, *The History of Witchcraft and Demonology*, 1926; Huggard's translation of Leibniz's *Theodicy*, 1951; Steiner's *Outline of Occult Science*, 1922 translation). Check the law where you live before redistributing them.
- **Greek texts.** The Greek of the Apocrypha (H. B. Swete, *The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint*) and of the Book of Enoch (Flemming and Radermacher, 1901) come from the Open Greek and Latin project and are licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Copies of those two texts must keep that licence and credit.
- **Amendments XVI–XXVII** of the United States Constitution are from the National Archives.
- **Cover images** are open-access (CC0) images from The Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Cleveland Museum of Art; each is credited in the library's `covers/credits.json`.
- **Illustrations** inside the books are from the same public-domain editions as their texts.
- **Translations made for this project** are marked as such in the reader.
