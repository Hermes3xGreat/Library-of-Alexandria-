Welcome to the Library of Alexandria

10 libraries and 793 works, all on this device. A few things worth knowing:

Libraries
Each door opens a library of its own. The Alexandria button at the bottom left of a library brings you back here.
Search
The search box finds a title or author in any library. You can then search inside every book for a word or phrase.
Versions
Where a work has a second translation or its original text, a switch at the top of the page changes between them.
Saved
Highlight a passage to keep it, add a note, or bookmark your place. Each library keeps its own.
